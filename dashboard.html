Why this candidate

Survival: no busts in ordinary matched stresses; a deliberately severe fat-tail 1.5x-volatility test exposed residual ruin risk.
Alternatives: use the 3.0% target for heavier-tail survival or the shifted t-stat rule for permanent signal reversal.
Important: the paired block-bootstrap improvement over the incumbent is not statistically conclusive. The adaptive-core benefit is stronger late in the sample, so this remains a controlled extrapolation, not proof.
